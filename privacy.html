Privacy

Last updated October 22, 2026

The short version: Chess Battle collects only what it needs to run your games, your nation and the leaderboards. There are no ads, no ad trackers, and your data is never sold.

What we store

  • A guest account. When you open Chess Battle it creates an anonymous account with a random ID. No signup, email or phone number is needed.
  • Your profile: the display name you choose (optional), your avatar, your nation, your legacy stars, rating, wins, losses and draws.
  • Your games: online and one-move-a-day games are saved on our server so they survive disconnects and can be watched live. This includes the board position, moves and result.
  • Friends: the friend connections you make with friend codes.
  • Activity: which days you opened the app, so we can measure whether players come back. This is counted, not tracked across other sites.
  • Email and password, only if you create an account. Your email is used to sign you in and to send password resets. Passwords are stored securely hashed by our database provider and are never visible to us.
  • A backup of your on-device progress (crowns, cosmetics, settings and stats) for signed-in players, so it follows you to every device.
  • On your device: settings, cosmetics, replays and progress are saved in your browser’s local storage.

What other players can see

Your display name, avatar, nation, legacy stars and win count appear on leaderboards, in your friends’ lists and to people watching your live games. Players under 13 never have their name shown publicly.

Who processes data

  • Supabase hosts the database and accounts.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website.
  • PeerJS may be used to connect two players directly when the server can’t be reached.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr / cdnjs deliver fonts and code libraries, which means they receive your IP address when the page loads.

Children

Chess is for everyone. When you first open the app we ask whether you are 13 or older. Players under 13 play with their name hidden from all public lists, and there is no free-text chat anywhere in Chess Battle, only preset reactions.

Deleting your data

Go to Profile → Account → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, profile, friends and saved games from the server. You can also clear everything on your device with Profile → Settings → Reset progress.

Contact
