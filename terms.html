Terms

Last updated October 22, 2026

Play fair, be kind, and have fun. Chess Battle is free to play, and everything you can buy is cosmetic.

Playing

  • Use one account for yourself. Don’t use extra accounts, bots or engines to win games, farm stars or move your nation up the standings.
  • Results that look manipulated, such as the same accounts trading wins, may not count, and the accounts involved can be removed.
  • Leaving a game you are losing counts as a loss once the claim time runs out.
  • Pick a display name that is not offensive or pretending to be someone else. We may reset names that break this rule.

Stars, crowns and cosmetics

Legacy stars, crowns, season rewards and nation standings are part of the game. They have no cash value and can’t be exchanged for money. We may rebalance them to keep the game fair.

The service

Chess Battle is provided as is. We work hard to keep it running, but games can occasionally be interrupted or lost. Features may change as the game grows.

Your content

Clips and share cards you make are yours to post anywhere. By playing online you allow your games to be shown to spectators and in replays.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/forestmack/chess-battle/issues.
